# Dashboard artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. These are decorative illustrations of a Red Mica crossover, not photographs of the owner's vehicle or source documents. Existing uploaded photos and PDFs remain in R2.

The generated PNG originals were retained in the generation library. The app uses WebP copies at their original 1536 × 1024 resolution, quality 80. All five optimized assets together are about 813 KiB. Images have explicit dimensions, and secondary pictures load lazily. Static artwork is cached with the app shell; API documents and reference photos retain their existing cache policy.

Loan & purchase and Car tips retain the original 3:2 image proportions in Overview cards and dashboard headers, keeping the complete car and foreground objects visible at every screen size.

## Assets and final prompts

### overview

App asset: `assets/dashboard-overview.webp`.

```
Create ONE polished automotive app illustration, no typography. This is the Overview picture for a personal car dashboard called Pika. Subject: a contemporary Toyota Corolla Cross hybrid compact SUV, deep Red Mica Metallic body, glossy crimson paint, dark tinted glass, graphite wheel arches and modern alloy wheels. It must look like a crossover SUV, not a sedan. Front three-quarter view with the entire car fully visible and a little space around it, pointing to the left. Scene: quiet curved road, minimalist warm ivory architectural garage, a few sculptural plants and distant soft hills. Premium photorealistic 3D editorial render, beautifully designed small diorama, realistic proportions, soft studio daylight, subtle grain, clean forms, burgundy accents and warm cream background. Compose as a wide horizontal 3:2 image; keep all essential content within the middle 70% of its height because the image will also be displayed as a shallow banner. The car is centered, slightly lower in frame, with generous negative space above and at the sides. No humans, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no readable license plate. Do not show a phone or app mockup. Deliver only the illustration.
```
